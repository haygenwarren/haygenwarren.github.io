A little about me

About

I’m Haygen Warren, a physics student at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. I’m focusing on astrophysics and expect to graduate in May 2027.

My work brings together astronomy, engineering, and communication. I develop astronomical data tools for Skynet Global Observatory, work on manufacturing and automation projects at Goshen Engineering, and lead news coverage at NASASpaceflight.

I also shoot film. This site is a place for my research, projects, photographs, and writing, with room to share what I’m learning along the way.
